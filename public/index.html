How verification & contact-finding works
  • Trading check: we fetch each dealer's website and look for their name in the footer, read off the registered company name & number, then look that company up on the free public Companies House website (no API key) to confirm it's active. Confidence reflects how many signals agreed.
  • Companies House: we also give you a one-click Google search of the company (name + number + "companies house") and a Companies House search link, so there's always a manual cross-check.
  • Officers: current directors are scraped from the company's public Companies House officers page, and each gets their own LinkedIn + Google search.
  • Contacts: LinkedIn forbids scraping and never exposes emails/phones, so we generate precise, ready-to-click LinkedIn & Google searches targeting Dealer Principals, Heads of Business, Sales Managers/Directors, MDs, Operations Directors, etc. Open them to read off names, then pull email/phone from the dealer's own contact page.
